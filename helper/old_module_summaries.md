# Old Secondary Module Summaries (Legacy System)

These are from the OLD system and are NOT backwards compatible with the current system. For reference only.

---

## Acolyte (acolyte.json)
Religious order member dedicated to divine or necromancy magic. Gains runecarving and arcanum, learns divine/necromancy spells, can craft sacred symbols with conduit slots, and has a dramatic death-defying Final Service ability. Fills a faith-based caster/crafter hybrid role.

**Key mechanics:** Spellcasting (divine/necromancy), crafting sacred symbols, Absolute Faith reaction (resilience/resistance bonus), Final Service (sacrifice for guaranteed survival on death check)

---

## Acrobat (acrobat.json)
Agility specialist focused on evasion and acrobatic combat. Reduces fall damage, replaces climbing with acrobatics, and gains a rolling dodge reaction that stacks on successive dodges. Can vault off enemies with Parkour Strike.

**Key mechanics:** Safe Fall (half fall damage), Rolling Dodge reaction (stacking dodge bonuses), Parkour Strike action (attack + bounce away), Quick Dodge (dodge vs rays)

---

## Arcanatech (arcanatech.json)
Cybernetic/magitech crafter who builds arcanatech enhancements and uses magical catalysts. Unlocks arcanatech crafting recipes, can install enhancements without removing body parts, and boosts crafted ranged weapons.

**Key mechanics:** Arcanatech crafting system, catalyst tier upgrades, exo-enhancements (non-surgical installation), ranged weapon infusion bonuses

---

## Arcanist (arcanist.json)
Raw magical power user who trades health for spell power. Can charge spells by taking psychic damage, empower allies' spells, prepare spells before combat, and challenge enemies to contested arcanum duels that inflict dazed.

**Key mechanics:** Arcanist Charge (self-damage to charge spells), Empower Ally reaction, Prepared Spell reaction (pre-combat casting), Mind Challenge action (contested arcanum, inflicts dazed)

---

## Armsman (armsman.json)
Versatile weapon master with stance-based combat. Opens with a heavy weapon attack that chains to a light weapon on miss, gains attack bonuses on misses, can swap stances as reactions, and equalizes all weapon attack modifiers to the highest.

**Key mechanics:** Diverting Strike (heavy + light weapon combo), Armsman Stance, Recurring Blows (miss = bonus on next attack), Change Stance reaction, Follow-up (melee + ranged combo)

---

## Assassin (assassin.json)
Stealth-based killer specializing in poison and hidden attacks. Coats weapons in deadly poison for instant toxic damage, can remain hidden after attacking, and has a devastating Assassinate action with massive bonus damage from stealth.

**Key mechanics:** Deadly Poison action (instant poison damage), From the Shadows (stay hidden after attack), Stealth Specialist (+2 attack from stealth), Assassinate (massive hidden damage, true hit)

---

## Athlete (athlete.json)
Physical fitness specialist with raw strength and endurance. Gains health, can use strength to resist grapples/shoves, boosts melee damage, and excels at a chosen might skill. Capstone grants defense across all three defensive checks.

**Key mechanics:** Bodybuilding (strength vs grapple/shove), Overpowering Strikes (+1 melee damage), Sprinter (move as free action), Mind and Body (+1 block/dodge/resistance)

---

## Barbarian (barbarian.json)
Unarmored melee berserker trading defense for offense. Cannot wear medium/heavy armor but gains physical mitigation and melee damage. Can turn true hits into maddened condition, retaliate after taking damage, and boost all mitigations with resilience.

**Key mechanics:** No medium/heavy armor restriction, Primal Fury reaction (downgrade true hit, become maddened), Retaliate reaction (counter-attack after damage), In the Thick action (resilience = mitigation bonus)

---

## Battledancer (battledancer.json)
Unarmed martial artist combining dance and combat. Uses performance and acrobatics for combat maneuvers, with Circle Kick (mobile attack), Sweeping Kick (prone + damage), and Lunging Kick (defend ally by dashing in).

**Key mechanics:** Circle Kick action (attack + circle movement), Sweeping Kick action (prone via performance vs acrobatics), Lunging Kick reaction (dash to ally + unarmed attack on their attacker)

---

## Battlemage (battlemage.json)
Armored spellcaster mixing melee combat with magic. Reduces armor casting penalties, automatically succeeds concentration checks, and can combo melee attacks into spells with Spell Combo.

**Key mechanics:** Quick Cast (reduce armor spell penalty), Endure reaction (auto-succeed concentration), Dual Training (+1 melee + spell attack), Spell Combo (hit with melee, then cast a spell)

## Beast Friend (beast_friend.json)
