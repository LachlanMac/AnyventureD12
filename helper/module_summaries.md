# Secondary Module Summaries

## Abjurer (ID: 999)
Defensive white magic specialist focused on protecting yourself and allies from dark, divine, and aetheric damage. Core mechanic is placing wards on allies and using magic knowledge as a substitute defense skill. Fills the magical tank/protector role.

**Key skills:** magic knowledge, might OR resilience, white magic, deflection OR academics+glyphcraft
**Key mechanics:** Action: White Ward (redirect damage from ally), Reaction: Shield (magic knowledge defense), Action: Protect (give ally your defense), Reaction: Divine Intervention (auto-succeed protection spell). Interacts with dark/divine/aetheric damage types. Shield-focused at 6a.

---

## Acolyte (ID: 999)
Religious sect member with access to secret knowledge and rituals. Splits between enhanced ritual casting (2a) and glyph inscription (2b), with deeper branching into white magic (Life Cult) or black magic (Death Cult). Fills a religious utility/support role.

**Key skills:** magic knowledge OR glyphcraft, persuasion, resilience, white OR black magic
**Key mechanics:** Daily Reaction: Conviction (reroll mind check with d20, risk/reward resolve), Ancient Knowledge (gain exotic school access), Daily Action: Offering (charge spell with health sacrifice). Dark/divine mitigation. Sect membership for social access.

---

## Acrobat (ID: 105)
Agile movement specialist focused on evasion, climbing, jumping, and fluid repositioning. Provides superior mobility in and out of combat with reactions that reward successful dodges. Fills the agile skirmisher/explorer role.

**Key skills:** coordination, stealth OR concentration, fitness OR coordination, evasion+thievery
**Key mechanics:** Reaction: Rolling Dodge (evasion with stacking bonus), Swift Climber (climb at full speed), Safe Fall (reduce fall damage), Daily: Escape Artist (auto-remove impaired), Parkour Master (+1 move, jump during sprint). No magic.

## Armsman (ID: 105)
Weapon versatility specialist who trains with many weapon types. Core mechanic revolves around using multiple weapons tactically, disarming, and using coordination as an attack skill. Fills the well-rounded weapon expert role.

**Key mechanics:** Action: Disarm (coordination contest), Action: Follow-Through (combo two weapons), Action: Find the Gap (bypass physical mitigation), Muscle Memory (negate first penalty dice per turn). No magic. Extra weapon slot usage.
