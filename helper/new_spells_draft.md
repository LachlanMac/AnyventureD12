# New Spells Draft (RC 15-16)

All spell RCs were shifted +2 globally. These are the new tier of spells being added.

## BLACK MAGIC

### Necromancy
**Embrace of Death** (RC 15)
- Base: You touch an adjacent creature and restore 10 of their health while setting their resolve to 0. For the next minute, they are immune to all mental conditions, stress, pain and they do not need to breathe. If the target of this spell is unconscious or asleep, they wake up. When the spell ends, the target must make a resilience check of 8. On a success, their resolve becomes 1. On a failure, they gain the broken condition. A creature can only be affected by this spell once per day.
- Charge: The creature is restored to full health and any bleeding or poisoned conditions are removed.
- Energy: 2
- Component: Embalming Fluid
- Range: 1 (Adjacent)

**Raise Compliant Dead** (RC 16)
- Base: You raise a humanoid corpse into a standard-tier compliant undead by placing two jade stones in the eye sockets. Choose a Compliant Skeleton Warrior, Compliant Risen, or Compliant Ghoul based on the condition of the corpse. The undead retains any equipment the corpse was wearing. A compliant undead is not maddened and is permanently loyal to the caster. It can follow complex non-combat orders and perform tasks to the best of its ability such as guarding, patrolling, carrying, and manual labor. It cannot perform tasks that require creativity or social interaction. There is no limit to the number of compliant undead a caster can maintain.
- Charge: You can raise the corpse as a champion-tier compliant undead instead (Compliant Skeleton Champion, Compliant Risen Champion, or Compliant Ghoul Champion).
- Energy: 5
- Component: Bone Iron Ingot
- Cast time: 10 minutes
- Range: 1 (Adjacent)

### Witchcraft
**Hex Chain** (RC 15)
- Base: You link two creatures within a short (10 unit) distance if your spell check beats both targets' resilience. This spell fails if there are not at least two valid targets. While you concentrate, whenever one linked creature takes dark damage, the other takes half of that damage as dark damage.
- Charge: You link up to four creatures instead of two.
- Energy: 2
- Component: Bonding Paste
- Range: 4 (Short)
- Concentration: Yes

**Wither** (RC 16)
- Base: You touch a creature and begin to rapidly age their body if your spell check beats the target's endurance. The spell lasts up to 4 rounds. At the start of each round, the target must make an endurance check against your original spell check. On failure, their physique, finesse, and weapon talents are each reduced by 1. The required check decreases by 2 each time. On success, the spell ends and the reductions fade. If the target fails all 4 checks, the reductions are permanent and can only be removed by a spell that can remove or cancel magic. Standard tier or lower creatures turn to dust.
- Charge: Elite tier creatures or lower can also be turned to dust on 4 failures.
- Energy: 4
- Component: Soul Sand
- Range: 1 (Adjacent)

### Fiend
**Infernal Gate** (RC 15)
- Base: You spend 10 minutes performing a ritual to open a small rift to the infernal plane. Roll a 1d20 to determine what answers: 1-10 nothing comes through, 11-16 a standard-tier devil, 17-19 a champion-tier devil, 20 an elite-tier devil. Standard-tier devils are under your control. Champion and elite-tier devils are not under your control and act on their own will. When a summoned devil reaches 0 health, it returns to its home plane. If you know a devil by name, you can attempt to summon it directly. If the devil is willing, the spell succeeds. If it is not willing, your spell check must beat double the devil's resilience. If nothing comes through or the spell fails to channel, the rift destabilizes and this spell cannot be cast again for 1d4 days.
- Charge: The formula changes to: 1-10 a standard-tier devil, 11-15 a champion-tier devil, 16+ an elite-tier devil. Champion-tier devils are also under your control.
- Energy: 5
- Components: Iron Idol, Fire Opal, Fire Opal
- Cast time: 10 minutes
- Range: 1 (Adjacent)

**Flame Portal** (RC 16)
- Base: You tear open a rift to the infernal plane, transporting yourself and all willing allies within a nearby (1 unit) distance to a point you can see within a moderate (20 unit) range. There must be enough unoccupied space at the destination to accommodate all transported creatures in the same formation. Upon departure and arrival, all enemies within a nearby (1 unit) distance of the rift take 10 heat damage. If you fail to channel this spell, the rift may open unpredictably, resulting in unintended and possibly dangerous effects with the infernal plane.
- Charge: The pickup and damage radius increase to a very short (5 unit) distance, and the heat damage increases to 15.
- Energy: 4
- Component: Fire Opal
- Range: 5 (Moderate)

## ARCANE MAGIC

### Chaos
**Gravity Spike** (RC 15)
- Base: You release a pulse of chaotic gravitational force centered on a point within short (10 unit) range with a very short (5 unit) radius. Choose a direction. All medium and smaller creatures in the area are displaced 5 units in the chosen direction. If a creature has something they could reasonably grab to anchor themselves within range, they can make a coordination check against your spell check to avoid being displaced. If a displaced creature collides with a surface or terrain, they must make a fitness check against your spell check or take 10 physical damage. If you do not roll at least 2 successes, the direction of the pulse is random.
- Charge: Large creatures are also affected.
- Energy: 4
- Component: Lodestone
- Range: 4 (Short)

**Aetherspike** (RC 16)
- Base: You target a creature within moderate (20 unit) range and drive a spike of chaotic energy through them, dealing 8 aetheric damage. For every other creature within a very short (5 unit) radius of the target, the damage increases based on their tier: minions and grunts add 1, standard creatures add 2, champion and higher add 3. If you do not roll at least 2 successes, the spike targets a random creature within range instead.
- Charge: The radius for counting additional creatures increases to short (10 units).
- Energy: 3
- Component: Void Dust
- Range: 5 (Moderate)

### Conjuration
**Arcane Binding** (RC 15)
- Base: You touch a piece of armor or shield you are wearing and bind it to yourself. The armor retains its stats but deals no encumbrance penalty while dismissed. You can summon or dismiss the armor as an action. The binding lasts until dispelled or until a new binding replaces it. If you do not roll at least 2 successes, the armor is destroyed unless it is crafted from aetherium parts.
- Charge: You can bind an entire set of armor (body, head, hands, boots) in a single casting. All bound pieces are summoned or dismissed together as one action. If you already have bound armor, you can channel this spell to gain +1 morale for each piece currently bound and +3 aetheric or psychic mitigation (chosen when cast) for the next day. This bonus can only be gained once per day.
- Energy: 3
- Component: Aetherium Nugget
- Cast time: 10 minutes
- Range: 0 (Self)

**Arcane Bastion** (RC 16)
- Base: You conjure a shimmering wall of aetheric energy within a very short (5 unit) radius centered on you while you maintain concentration. When a projectile or area spell targets a creature within the wall, the wall takes the damage instead. You must then make an arcane magic check against half the damage dealt or the wall shatters. Creatures you designate can pass through the wall freely. While you maintain this spell, you cannot move or take other actions.
- Charge: You can move and take other actions while maintaining the wall.
- Energy: 4
- Component: Alexandrite
- Range: 0 (Self)
- Concentration: Yes

### Enchantment
**Empower** (RC 15)
- Base: You touch a creature and enhance one of their skills. Choose a skill. That skill becomes 3d12 until the end of the target's next full rest. A creature can only benefit from one instance of this spell at a time.
- Charge: All skills under the chosen skill's attribute become 3d16 until the end of the target's next full rest.
- Energy: 2
- Component: Pearl
- Range: 1 (Adjacent)

**Puppet** (RC 16)
- Base: You touch a grunt or lower tier creature and completely dominate their mind if your spell check beats their resilience. The target is utterly charmed and obeys your commands without question, including commands that would cause self-harm. The effect lasts for 1 hour. Creatures immune to the charmed condition are not affected.
- Charge: The effect is permanent until dispelled by magic, and can target standard tier creatures.
- Energy: 3
- Component: Diatessite
- Range: 1 (Adjacent)

## WHITE MAGIC

### Protection
**Hallowed Ground** (RC 15)
- Base: You place a platinum idol on the ground and consecrate the area within a very short (5 unit) radius around it. Choose two conditions from the following: afraid, charmed, confused, maddened, ignited, blinded, or deafened. For the next hour, allies within the area are immune to the chosen conditions. Any ally currently suffering from a chosen condition is freed of it upon entering the area. All attacks and spells channeled against you or your allies within the area are rolled with a penalty die. The area is centered on the idol and does not move.
- Charge: The effect is permanent as long as the idol remains in place. If the idol is touched, moved, or destroyed, the effect ends immediately and the idol is destroyed.
- Energy: 3
- Component: Platinum Idol
- Cast time: 1 minute
- Range: 1 (Adjacent)

**Exorcise** (RC 16)
- Base: You touch a creature. If the target is a willing ally, you remove any dark spells, curses, or possession affecting them if your spell check beats the RC of the original effect. Additionally, the target rolls with a bonus die on all checks against dark magic for the next minute, or until they succeed on one such check. If the target is a standard or lower tier undead or dark creature, it is destroyed.
- Charge: Undead or dark creatures of champion tier or lower are destroyed.
- Energy: 4
- Component: Platinum Idol
- Range: 1 (Adjacent)

### Radiant
**Mend Soul** (RC 15)
- Base: You touch a creature and restore 5 health and 5 resolve. If the target has the broken condition, the required check to recover is lowered by 1. A creature can only benefit from this spell once per day.
- Charge: The restoration increases to 10 health and 10 resolve, and the spell affects all allies within a very short (5 unit) radius instead of a single target.
- Energy: 3
- Component: Moonstone
- Range: 1 (Adjacent)

**Gift of Life** (RC 16)
- Base: You touch a humanoid or beast that has died within the last day and attempt to restore their life. The body must be mostly intact. The target must roll a 1d12; on a 1, the creature can no longer be a target of this spell. Each previous successful resurrection increases the required roll by 1. If successful, the creature returns to life with 1 health, 0 resolve, and gains the broken condition.
- Charge: The time limit extends to 7 days, and the creature returns with half their maximum health instead of 1 and does not gain the broken condition.
- Energy: 5
- Component: Diamond
- Cast time: 1 hour
- Range: 1 (Adjacent)

### Celestial
**Radiant Beam** (RC 15)
- Base: You unleash a beam of radiant energy in a 1-unit wide line up to short (10 unit) distance, dealing 15 heat and 15 divine damage to all creatures in the path whose defense is beaten by your spell check.
- Charge: The beam only affects undead and dark creatures but deals 40 divine damage instead.
- Energy: 4
- Component: Platinum Shavings
- Range: 4 (Short)

**Divine Guidance** (RC 16)
- Base: You make a formal petition to a divine power, seeking guidance on a matter of importance. Roll a d100. 1-50: No answer. 51-75: Vague guidance in the form of a cryptic vision, feeling, or omen. 76-90: Clear guidance in the form of a direct and understandable answer or direction. 91-100: Divine intervention in the form of direct action, a miracle, or an unmistakable sign that alters the situation in your favor. This spell can only be cast once per day.
- Charge: 1-50: Vague guidance. 51-85: Clear guidance. 86-100: Divine intervention.
- Energy: 4
- Component: Gold Idol
- Cast time: 1 minute
- Range: 0 (Self)

## MYSTICISM

### Spirit
**Soulbind** (RC 15)
- Base: You touch a creature and anchor their soul to the current location. If the target dies within the next 24 hours, their soul lingers at the anchored location for 1d6 days. If the target's body is brought within a very short (5 unit) distance of the soul while it still lingers, and the body is mostly intact, the soul returns to the body if the target rolls a 2 or higher on a 1d10. On a 1, the spell fails and the soul cannot be returned to the body by this spell. The creature revives with 1 health and 0 resolve. You can only have one soulbind active at a time.
- Charge: The soulbind is permanent until the caster dies. The soul lingers for 1d10 days instead of 1d6.
- Energy: 3
- Component: Spirit Moonstone
- Cast time: 10 minutes
- Range: 1 (Adjacent)

**Soul Tap** (RC 16)
- Base: You touch a creature and tap into their soul, dealing 10 true damage if your spell check beats the target's resilience. As part of the same action, you can transfer half the damage dealt as health to another creature of the same type (humanoid to humanoid, beast to beast) within a very short (5 unit) distance.
- Charge: You can heal any creature regardless of the type of your target.
- Energy: 3
- Component: Amethyst Dust
- Range: 1 (Adjacent)

### Divination
**Flow Step** (RC 15)
- Base: You enter a trance at your current location and step back in time to observe past events that occurred there. You choose how far back to look, up to 1 year. Your body enters stasis while your awareness travels to that moment. You can observe events as they unfolded in real time from your fixed position but cannot interact with anything. The vision lasts up to 10 minutes of real time.
- Charge: You can look back up to 100 years and bring up to 2 willing allies into the vision with you.
- Energy: 3
- Component: Soul Sand
- Cast time: 10 minutes
- Range: 0 (Self)

**Temporal Fracture** (RC 16)
- Base: You target a creature within short (10 unit) distance and fracture their timeline if your spell check beats the target's resilience. The creature takes 1d4 true damage for each round of combat that has elapsed. The wounds appear as echoes of attacks from throughout the battle, materializing across the target's body simultaneously.
- Charge: The spell affects all creatures within a very short (5 unit) radius instead of a single target.
- Energy: 4
- Component: Alexandrite
- Range: 4 (Short)

### Cosmic
**Dimension** (RC 15)
- Base: You touch a small object with an interior (a locket, box, chest, bottle) and create a pocket dimension within it that lasts for 1 day. The interior space is a 10x10x10 unit room with solid ground, breathable air, and soft light. You and creatures you permit can use an action while touching the object to enter the dimension. Only one creature can exit per round, appearing in an adjacent space at the start of the next round. If the object is destroyed, the dimension collapses. Creatures inside are ejected and take 10 true damage. Objects inside are lost.
- Charge: The dimension is permanent until the object is destroyed or the magic is removed. The dimension retains its contents between uses.
- Energy: 3
- Component: Star Ruby
- Cast time: 10 minutes
- Range: 1 (Adjacent)

**Rift Arrow** (RC 16)
- Base: You call upon the void and launch an arrow of rift energy at a creature you can see, regardless of distance. Your spell check is rolled against a single d20. If the arrow hits, the target takes 25 aetheric damage. The arrow remains lodged in the creature, preventing them from moving or being displaced. The target or an adjacent ally can use an action to remove the arrow with a might check of 10.
- Charge: On hit, the target is immediately pulled into the void. They can use their action to attempt to pull the arrow out with a might check of 14. When the arrow is removed, they return to the space they were taken from. If the arrow is not removed within 1 minute, the creature is lost to the void permanently.
- Energy: 5
- Component: Void Amethyst
- Range: 8 (Planar)

## PRIMAL MAGIC

### Elemental
**Boulder** (RC 15)
- Base: You conjure a massive boulder above a creature within short (10 unit) range and let it fall. If your spell check beats the target's coordination, they take 20 true damage. If this damage brings the target to 0 health, they are crushed and killed instantly.
- Charge: The boulder remains on the battlefield as a 1-tile obstacle with 30 HP until destroyed.
- Energy: 4
- Component: Nickel Nugget
- Range: 4 (Short)

**Chain Lightning** (RC 16)
- Base: You target a creature within moderate (20 unit) range and strike them with a devastating bolt of lightning, dealing 40 electric damage if your spell check beats the target's defense. The bolt then arcs to the nearest creature within a very short (5 unit) distance of the last target hit, dealing 5 less damage with each arc. The bolt arcs a number of times equal to the number of successes on your spell check. A creature can only be hit once by this spell.
- Charge: The initial damage increases to 50 and the arc range increases to short (10 units).
- Energy: 5
- Component: Storm Topaz
- Range: 5 (Moderate)

### Nature
**Protection of the Grove** (RC 15)
- Base: You designate a natural area within a short (10 unit) radius as a protected grove for the next 8 hours. Hostile beasts and plantoids cannot willingly enter the grove. Beasts and plantoids already within the area when the spell is cast become non-hostile and cooperative with the caster, following simple commands. Creatures that complete a full rest within the grove gain the benefits of a favorable rest regardless of conditions. The grove must be in a natural environment (forest, jungle, grassland, cave with natural growth).
- Charge: The grove becomes permanent but you can only maintain one permanent grove at a time. Creating a new permanent grove ends the previous one. The emerald becomes the heart of the grove. If it is removed or destroyed, the grove is destroyed. While within your permanent grove, you can shape trees, roots, and vegetation at will, forming natural shelters, furniture, and barriers. Beasts and plantoids within the grove obey your commands and act as guardians, alerting you to intruders.
- Energy: 3
- Component: Emerald
- Cast time: 10 minutes
- Range: 0 (Self)

**Reclaim** (RC 16)
- Base: You touch the ground and nature begins to slowly reclaim an area within a short (10 unit) radius. Non-magical structures, walls, and metallic objects in the area slowly deteriorate and are destroyed over the next minute. Equipped items are not affected.
- Charge: Constructs within the area must make a resilience check against your spell check or take 20 true damage. Inactive constructs are destroyed by this spell.
- Energy: 5
- Component: Concentrated Quicksilver
- Cast time: 1 minute
- Range: 0 (Self)

### Draconic
**Edict: Silence** (RC 15)
- Base: You speak the draconic word for silence and all sound ceases within a very short (5 unit) radius centered on you for the next minute. All creatures in the area are muted and deafened. Spells channeled within the area are rolled with a penalty die. Sound cannot enter or leave the area. Songs and ballads being performed within the area immediately end. You are immune to these effects.
- Charge: The radius increases to short (10 units). Creatures within the area that are concentrating on a spell or performing a song must make a concentration check against your spell check or lose their concentration.
- Energy: 4
- Component: Treant Core
- Range: 0 (Self)

**Dragonheart** (RC 16)
- Base: You touch a humanoid or beast that has died within the last hour and channel the ancient power of the dragons to restore their life. The creature returns with 1 health, 0 resolve, and gains the broken condition. Your maximum health is permanently reduced by 1. Your life force is permanently bound to the target. If the target ever dies again, you also die.
- Charge: The caster does not lose maximum health. Any missing limbs or body parts on the target are replaced with golden draconic replicas that function identically to the originals, granting the creature +1 mitigation to the caster's heritage element for each replaced part. The creature returns at half health and does not gain the broken condition. The life bond still applies.
- Energy: 5
- Component: 1000 gold (consumed in the ritual, transmuted into the golden replicas)
- Cast time: 1 hour
- Range: 1 (Adjacent)

## META MAGIC

### Transmutation
**Mattermorph** (RC 15)
- Base: You touch a champion or lower tier creature and attempt to transform them into a tiny non-magical object that you have in your possession if your spell check beats the target's endurance. While transformed, the creature is aware but cannot act, move, or communicate. The object is unnaturally durable, having 15 HP and immunity to non-magical damage. The spell lasts for 1 minute, or until the object is destroyed or the effect is dispelled. If the object is destroyed, the creature reforms and takes 10 true damage. Willing creatures can be transformed without a check.
- Charge: The tier limit is removed. Your spell check is rolled against double the target's endurance, but the transformation is permanent until dispelled by magic.
- Energy: 4
- Component: Flux Powder
- Range: 1 (Adjacent)

**Flight** (RC 16)
- Base: You grant yourself a magical flight speed of 8 units for the next minute.
- Charge: All willing allies within a very short (5 unit) radius also gain a magical flight speed of 8 units for the next minute.
- Energy: 3
- Component: Zephyr Dust
- Range: 0 (Self)

### Illusion
**Mirage** (RC 15)
- Base: You mark an area with a very short (5 unit) radius centered on a point you touch. While you are within the area and concentrating, you can use an action to alter one aspect of the environment (a wall, the floor, a doorway, furniture, lighting, sounds, smells). Each alteration affects sight, sound, smell, and touch. Alterations persist until you change them or the spell ends. A creature can see through an individual alteration if their senses or logic beats your original spell check. Leaving the area or losing concentration ends the spell.
- Charge: The radius increases to short (10 units). The illusion no longer ends if you leave the area. If the mirage remains uninterrupted and you concentrate for at least 1 hour, it becomes permanent until dispelled by magic that removes illusions.
- Energy: 3
- Component: Amethyst Dust
- Range: 1 (Adjacent)
- Concentration: Yes

**Copycat** (RC 16)
- Base: While you concentrate on this spell, you exist in a state of illusory flux. You gain the following abilities:
  - Reaction (1 energy): When targeted by a melee attack, double your defense check against the triggering attack. If you successfully avoid the attack, you become invisible until the start of your next turn.
  - Action (1 energy): Create a copy of yourself in your space. You and the copy each immediately move up to 2 units. You cannot end your turn occupying the same space as a copy. The copy lasts until the start of your next turn. Enemies cannot deduce which is the real you. A creature can see through the copy if their senses or logic beats your original spell check.
- Charge: When using the action, you create two copies instead of one. You and both copies each move up to 2 units.
- Energy: 2
- Component: Opal
- Range: 0 (Self)
- Concentration: Yes

### Fey
**Prism Prison** (RC 15)
- Base: You target a creature within a short (10 unit) distance and encase them in shimmering bands of prismatic light if your spell check beats the target's coordination. Standard tier and lower creatures are incapacitated. Champion and higher tier creatures are impaired. While imprisoned, the creature can use an action to attempt a might or coordination check against your original spell check to break free. On a failed attempt, the creature takes 5 psychic damage. The prison lasts as long as you concentrate.
- Charge: Champion tier and lower creatures are incapacitated instead of impaired. Failed escape attempts deal 10 aetheric damage instead of 5 psychic.
- Energy: 3
- Component: Prismatic Quartz
- Range: 4 (Short)
- Concentration: Yes

**Enthrall** (RC 16)
- Base: You target a creature within a short (10 unit) distance and captivate their mind with fey magic if your spell check beats the target's concentration. The target becomes stunned and is completely unaware of their surroundings for as long as you concentrate. Taking damage or being physically disturbed ends the effect. A creature can only be affected by this spell once per day.
- Charge: The spell affects all creatures within a short (10 unit) radius instead of a single target.
- Energy: 4
- Component: Dreamsand
- Range: 4 (Short)
- Concentration: Yes
