# Wrestler (UA)

Secondary module. Grappling specialist who controls enemies through holds, throws, and submissions.

## Identity
- The grapple master. Default grapple exists but nobody enhances it.
- Pins, holds, throws, reversal counters, slam attacks on grappled/prone targets
- Uses might/fitness offensively in a way Bruiser and Bonebreaker don't
- Non-magical

## Design Space
- Enhanced grapple (penalty to grappled creature's attacks)
- Grapple larger creatures (normally limited by size)
- Combined shove + prone (powershove)
- Reversal reaction (counter enemy grapple/shove attempts)
- Slam action (devastating attack vs grappled/prone targets)
- Submission holds (sustained grapple for escalating damage or conditions)
- Throw (use grappled creature as projectile or displace into hazards)
- Choke-outs (sustained grapple leads to unconscious)
- Suplex/body slam (take damage yourself but deal more)

## Existing Grapple/Prone Coverage (don't overlap)
- Bruiser T7 Curbstomp: devastating attack vs prone (might as attack)
- Bonebreaker T6a Seismic Slam: AoE prone
- BattleDancer T2a Sweeping Kick: expression-based prone
- Juggernaut: shove specialist (displacement, wall slams, bulldozer)
- Bounty Hunter T3: enhanced grab
- Bulwark T2a Solid Footing: can't be displaced/knocked prone while defending
