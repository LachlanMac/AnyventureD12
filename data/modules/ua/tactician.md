# Tactician (UA)

Secondary module. Battlefield commander who directs allies through positioning, coordination, and strategic combat.

## Identity
- The "move your friends around the board" module
- Non-magical leadership through tactical superiority
- Distinct from Mentor (teaches skills 1-on-1), Knight (protects allies, squire), Skald (inspires through music)
- Deeper than Leader personality module (which has Rally at T4b and Tactical Reposition at T6b)

## Design Space
- Repositioning multiple allies (move them tactically)
- Coordinated flanking bonuses when allies surround targets
- Granting allies extra actions or reactions through commands
- Formation-based defense bonuses (adjacent allies gain mitigation or defense)
- Sharing/manipulating initiative order
- Ordering specific attacks on specific targets for bonuses
- Reading the battlefield (academics/logic for tactical advantage)
- Signal system (horn, hand signals) for coordination at range

## Existing Overlap to Avoid
- Leader personality T4b Rally: doubles ally free movement toward you
- Leader personality T6b Tactical Reposition: prep phase, move 3 allies 2 units
- Loyalist personality T4b Coordinated Flank: you + ally attack same target
- Skald T4a Inspiring Rally: grant ally an out-of-turn action
- Knight T5 Knight's Mantle: give ally defend action
- Mentor: broad skill buffing through teaching
- Scout T3 Combat Coordinator: share initiative with ally
