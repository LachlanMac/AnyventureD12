# Mesmerist (UA)

Secondary module. Magical mind controller/healer. The "charmed condition specialist" like Fearmonger is the "afraid specialist."

## Identity
- Uses arcane magic (enchantment subschool) to directly control minds
- Distinct from Illusionist (tricks senses) - Mesmerist controls what you THINK/FEEL/DO
- Distinct from Counselor (non-magical therapy) - Mesmerist is magical, faster, can also go offensive
- Distinct from Diplomat (social persuasion) - Mesmerist uses magic-enhanced mental pressure

## Confirmed Mechanics

### Hypnotherapy (T1, passive)
- Target must be charmed (willing ally enters trance, enemy must already have condition)
- Roll arcane magic check, restore OR reduce resolve by half the result
- Once per rest per target
- Takes ~10 minutes
- Offensive use: interrogation tool. Charm someone, drain their resolve, break their will
- Healing use: magical therapy. Faster than Counselor's rest-based sessions

### Mind Swap (T4a, action)
- Touch a creature that has a mental condition (charmed/afraid/maddened/confused)
- Swap it to a different mental condition of your choice
- If the creature is immune to the new condition, they retain the original
- Great team synergy (Fearmonger inflicts afraid, you swap to charmed)
- Requires touch for balance
