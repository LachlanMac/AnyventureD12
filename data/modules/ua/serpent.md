# Serpent (UA)

Secondary module. Poison combat specialist with snake-themed agility and venom mastery.

## Identity
- The "poison condition specialist" like Fearmonger is for afraid, Bloodmage is for bleeding (self)
- Snake-strike combat style using coordination/initiative offensively
- Builds on the existing alchemy poison system (coat weapon, endurance check, descending RC)
- Non-magical

## Design Space
- Making poisons harder to shake (raise/maintain RC, slow the descent)
- Stacking multiple poisons on one target
- Extracting venom from venomous creatures (component harvesting)
- Bonus attacks/damage vs poisoned targets
- Initiative-based combat (fast strikes, using initiative as defense)
- Coiled lunge (gap closer attacks)
- Disabling venom (poisons cause extra conditions like exhaustion or impaired)
- Toxic mitigation for self (handling poisons safely)

## Existing Poison Coverage (don't overlap)
- Assassin T6a Poison Master: first poison damage doubled
- Bounty Hunter T6a: enhanced poison darts
- Naturalist T6a Toxicologist: enhanced antidotes, +1 alchemy
- Sporekeeper: toxic spore mechanics (biosculpting, not alchemy)

## Poison System Reference
- Consumable alchemy crafts, ingested or coated on weapon
- Target makes endurance check vs RC (usually 8-9)
- RC decreases by 1 each round, clears on success
- Types: toxic damage, dark damage, impaired condition, sleep
- Vial of Venom is a crafting component (250g, uncommon)
