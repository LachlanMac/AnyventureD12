# Arcane Marksman (UA)

Secondary module. Magic-infused ranged combatant who channels spells through projectiles.

## Identity
- The ranged equivalent of War Mage (War Mage is melee spell-weapon, this is ranged spell-weapon)
- Could be arcane archer, gun mage, or spellshot depending on direction
- Possibly combines magismith crafting (enchanting ranged weapons/ammo) with arcane shooting
- Uses arcane magic + ranged weapon skills

## Design Ideas
- Infuse projectiles with spell effects (arrow of sleep, bolt of fire, etc.)
- Craft or enchant ranged weapons with permanent magical properties
- Use arcane magic skill to enhance ranged attacks
- Guided projectiles (curve arrows around cover, homing shots)
- Elemental ammunition (convert ranged damage to spell damage type)
- Spell delivery through projectiles (cast a spell, fire it as an arrow at range)
- Wand-gun hybrid (a crafted weapon that IS a conduit)
- Could use engineering or fabrication alongside arcane magic

## Key Question
How different is this from just using a wand/staff? The appeal is physical projectiles enhanced by magic, not pure spell attacks. The archer who makes their arrows explode, freeze, or seek targets. The gunslinger who builds a magic firearm. The crossbowman whose bolts carry enchantments.

## Existing Ranged + Magic Coverage
- War Mage: melee Spellstrike, armored casting. NOT ranged.
- Warlock: staves/wands, mana system, Blast action. Pure magic attacks, not physical projectiles.
- Elementalist T4b Elemental Strikes: convert MELEE to elemental. Not ranged.
- Sniper: pure physical ranged, zero magic
- Pavise Guard: shield + ranged, zero magic
- Skirmisher: mobile ranged, zero magic
- No module currently lets you channel spells through ranged weapon attacks
