# Resting

Resting allows characters to recover health and resolve between adventures. There are two types of rest based on the conditions and duration.

Whenever a rule refers to a **full rest**, it means completing either kind of rest, favorable or unfavorable. It only has to be finished, not taken in good conditions.

## Favorable Rest

A favorable rest requires **8 hours of uninterrupted sleep** in comfortable, safe conditions. If a character wakes up during this period, the rest is no longer considered favorable.

### Conditions for Favorable Rest
- Inns or taverns with proper beds
- Safe houses or homes
- Well-established camps with proper shelter
- Temples or healing houses

### Benefits
- Recover **3 health**
- Gain **1 resolve**

## Unfavorable Rest

An unfavorable rest is **6 hours of sleep within a 10 hour period**. This represents broken or uncomfortable sleep.

### Conditions for Unfavorable Rest
- Sleeping on the ground or in a bedroll
- Tents in the wilderness
- Exposed to weather elements
- Unsafe or hostile territory
- Any interrupted sleep

### Benefits
- Recover **2 health**
- Lose **2 morale**

## Rest Rolls

Good gear can turn a rough night into a proper rest. If a character completes **8 hours of uninterrupted sleep** that would otherwise be an unfavorable rest, and they have gear that adds to the rest roll, they roll a d100 and add the bonus from their bedroll and shelter. **On a total of 100 or higher, the rest is favorable instead.**

| Gear | Rest roll bonus |
|------|-----------------|
| Bedroll | +0 |
| Comfortable Bedroll | +20 |
| Luxurious Bedroll | +40 |
| Tent | +20 |
| Luxurious Tent | +40 |

Only one bedroll and one shelter count. If the sleep was interrupted or shorter than 8 hours, gear cannot help.

<div class="example-box">
A scout camps on a rocky ridge in a tent, sleeping in a comfortable bedroll, and sleeps 8 hours without waking. The ground and weather would normally make this an unfavorable rest, so they make a rest roll. The tent and bedroll each add +20. They roll a 71 on the d100, add 40 for a total of 111, and wake rested with a favorable rest instead.
</div>
